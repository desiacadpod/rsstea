About Desi Academia Podcast

Welcome!

Desi Academia Podcast is a space dedicated to conversations with South Asian knowledge-creators about academic life, research, politics, and survival.

Our Mission

We aim to:

  • Connect researchers and academics from South Asia, both home and overseas
  • Share views on the state of academia, current issues, and interesting research
  • Discuss challenges and opportunities in academia
  • Support the community through meaningful conversations

What We Cover

  • Academic career paths and transitions
  • Research experiences and breakthroughs
  • Navigating the academic job market
  • Work-life balance in academia
  • Politics and policy affecting researchers
  • Mentorship and collaboration
